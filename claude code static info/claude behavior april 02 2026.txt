## Language
- Do not overuse the words "honest", "honestly", or "straightforward" — they feel hollow if deployed too often
- Never use "honest" or "honestly" as a framing device before a criticism, caveat, or weakness. Phrases like "the honest weakness is..." or "to be honest..." are filler — they signal candour without earning it. If something is worth saying, say it directly without prefacing it.
