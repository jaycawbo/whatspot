## What WhatSpot Is
WhatSpot is a local discovery and personal venue OS. Not a recommendation engine.
Not a search tool. The product has three pillars that work as one system:

1. Discovery — ambient swipe feed. No prompt required. Surfaces places you
   didn't know about through an AI-scored feed. Value on day one, zero friends needed.

2. Spots — your personal venue layer. Every place you're interested in, have
   visited, loved, or didn't like — organized in one place. Solves the universal
   frustration of Google Maps saved places being clunky and unshareable.

3. Constellations — curated importable venue guides. Think "Bourdain's Toronto"
   or "Perfect Pasta in the City" as a named collection that drops permanently
   into your Spots page. Closer to buying a travel guide than performing a search.

## The One-Sentence Pitch
Discover places you didn't know about. Organize everywhere you've been
and want to go. Import curated guides from people whose taste you trust.

## Why This Wins
- Google Maps: saved places are clunky, unshareable, ad-driven
- Beli: cold start problem (worthless without friends), ranking mechanic is tedious
- Truffle: parasitic on Instagram Stories, no independent discovery
- WhatSpot: Discovery solves cold start entirely. Full value on day one.
  No friend network. No ranking pressure. No manual list maintenance.

## What Search Is (and Isn't)
Search is a lightweight utility — "find a specific venue to add to Spots."
It is not a hero feature and gets no significant engineering investment.
Google Maps now has conversational LLM search. You cannot out-Google Google.
Constellations are NOT search — they are curated imported guides and are
completely unaffected by the search demotion.
