## What WhatSpot Is
AI-powered local discovery app (Toronto-first). Replicates the 2-hour research
process of tab-switching between Google, Reddit, critics, social. Conversational
search + multi-source consensus scoring. Not an aggregator — feels like a
recommendation from a credible local friend.
